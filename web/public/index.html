Why Aside

A translation is thousands of tiny chances to crash a game.

One unclosed {i}, one mistyped [name], one line too long for the box. Aside catches them while you write, not after a player reports them.

In a text editor the usual way

  • You find a broken tag when the game throws an exception.
  • You guess who says a line and what is on screen.
  • A choice is a lone string, cut off from what follows it.
  • Progress lives in a spreadsheet you forget to update.
  • Texts the generator skipped stay in English.

In Aside the calm way

  • Every line is checked against its original as you type.
  • The speaker, the scene and the lines around it sit next to the text.
  • The Menu tab shows the question, every option and what follows it.
  • Progress per line, file and episode, a weekly goal, a finish estimate.
  • Missed texts are found and added once you approve them.

FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? The documentation goes deeper, or write to the author.

Does Aside change my game?
No. Game folders are only read. Aside edits the translation files in game/tl/<language>, always after a backup, and keeps its own data in a small .aside folder next to them.
Which games work?
Ren'Py 7.4 and 8 games, with or without a tl folder and with .rpa archives. It was tried on 8 real games with 9 to 687 script files. The scene preview is best on games made of full-screen renders.
Is it only for Czech?
No. Any language works. The interface is in English, and language packs add the rules of a language (Czech is the first). Without a pack the neutral rules apply.
Do I need the Ren'Py SDK?
No. Aside unpacks archives itself and uses the game's own engine to generate translation files. The SDK is only needed if you want to run Ren'Py's lint from Aside.
What is the catch with Free?
There is none. The whole translating workflow is free. Pro sells time savers for big projects, and you can try them on 20 lines at a time.
Why will Windows warn me about the installer?
The first builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. Each file comes with a SHA-256 checksum.
